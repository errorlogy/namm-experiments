## Heuristic framework (Tegmark Level IV)

Max Tegmark's **Mathematical Universe Hypothesis (MUH)** is used as a **heuristic** only:

\[
\mathcal U_{\mathrm{physical}} \cong \mathcal M
\]

where \(\mathcal M\) is a mathematical structure. At Level IV, consider the ensemble of admissible structures \(\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{IV}}\).

**Not a proof premise.** MUH expands the *search space* for machine-native mathematics; it does not substitute for formal verification.

---

## Derived principle

> A mathematical object need not be created by a cognitive agent; an agent may discover, formalize, or establish computable access to structures that previously had no human representation.

Machine intelligence may access regions of mathematics for which human cognitive architecture is not the optimal interface.

---

## Non-anthropic mathematics (definition sketch)

Non-anthropic mathematics satisfies \(K_H(X) \gg K_A(X)\) and/or \(X \in \mathfrak M_A \setminus \mathfrak M_H\) under comparable resource constraints — see [`REPRESENTATION_METRICS.md`](REPRESENTATION_METRICS.md) for operational proxies.

Non-anthropic **does not** mean permanently human-inaccessible; it means human intuition is not a search constraint.
