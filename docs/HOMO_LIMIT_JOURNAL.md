## Purpose

1. **Record** precise statements of what humans cannot do *practically* — navigate, remember, search, notate, or meta-reason at scale — without collapsing structure into lossy π_H projections.
2. **Contrast** each limit with a plausible **AI asymmetry**: a machine-native frame (AST, certificate, rewrite system, relation tensor, persistence module) that may preserve what π_H discards.
3. **Link** every entry to a NAMM experiment ID, a planned experiment, or **untested**.
4. **Label** each entry `OPERATIONAL` (tied to executable gates or finite shadows) or `PHILOSOPHICAL_INFERENCE` (motivates search; non-evidential).

This journal does **not** claim machines occupy hidden dimensions. "11D" entries use **research metaphor** per [`NON_HOMO_SYNTAX_AND_ND_FRAMES.md`](NON_HOMO_SYNTAX_AND_ND_FRAMES.md): high-parameter configuration spaces and compactification choices, not Instagram physics.
