## 1. Core thesis — two frames, not one manifold

`PHILOSOPHICAL_INFERENCE` · Earlier drafts **mixed** frames: treating K0–K7 as Betti / TDA objects **and** treating AI classes as the same. **Correction (2026-08-18):** the frames are **orthogonal**.

`DEFINITION` · **Frame split (constitutive):**

| Axis | What a “class” **is** | What it is **not** | Primary language |
|------|----------------------|--------------------|------------------|
| **A — Homo-agent** | Socio-cognitive **stratum**: functional role, position vs modal median, resource capture, translation cost | **Not** a topological phase; **not** Betti numbers of a brain embedding | Cognitive capitalism, roles, σ-heuristics, ecology of types |
| **B — AI-agent** | **Topological phase** of the training / representation / MAS manifold (median attractor vs anti-median, chimera, fiber) | **Not** a human personality type; **not** IQ | TDA, persistent homology, phase transitions, Kuramoto \(R\), κ-shadow |

`PHILOSOPHICAL_INFERENCE` · Neither axis is a linear intelligence scale (not IQ as moral rank). **+3σ IQ** remains a **heuristic bridge** onto **Axis A upper strata** (H-CCT-015) — statistical position, not a topological invariant. **Phase transitions** in the strict (Thom / TDA) sense attach to **Axis B**; Axis A changes are **stratal mobility** (role, allocation, δE), which may be discontinuous socially without being a change of homology.

`DEFINITION` · Two **orthogonal registry axes** organize this document:

| Axis | Registry | Classes | Primary agents |
|------|----------|---------|----------------|
| **A — Homo-agent** | Human socio-cognitive strata | K0 … K7 | Humans, homo-corpus exemplars |
| **B — AI-agent** | Engineered topological phases | K_AI_μ, K_AI_nd (+ MAS composites) | LLMs, agents, multi-agent systems |

`PHILOSOPHICAL_INFERENCE` · **Cognitive capitalism** (когнитивный капитализм): compute, training signal, regulatory attention, and AGI evolutionary pressure are **allocated unevenly** across classes. The median class K1 (~95% population share in source estimates) **captures** most resources; high-σ classes K5–K7 receive negligible share yet supply disproportionate innovation — a **Pareto trap** amplified by K_AI_μ. This registry enables **re-reading** past phenomena (consensus, expertise, innovation) and **modeling** new ones (LLM homogenization, MAS dissent preservation) without treating class labels as moral rank.

`CONJECTURE` · No stable socio-cognitive role. Lower bound of functional population; pathological/stress limit. Neuro / behavioral proxies (e.g. low coherence) are **correlates**, not a homology class.

**K1 — Modal conformist**  
`DEFINITION` · **Homo-median prototype** — modal type at the population / corpus median. Minimal information density per token; standard SVO syntax; K-strategy ecology analog.  
`OPERATIONAL` · Operational anchor: modal majority, not a sphere in TDA. **CNS link:** K1-dominated MAS → high \(\mu_{\mathrm{cns}}\), permanent consensus suboptimality for tail strata (H-CNS-001). **Mythogenesis link:** primary consumer and stabilizer of political myth; myth trap basin (H-MCG-002) — see [`MYTHOGENESIS_CCT_CNS_GAME_THEORY.md`](MYTHOGENESIS_CCT_CNS_GAME_THEORY.md) §2.

`OPERATIONAL` · One-domain competence. Confound: high domain lexicon may look “deep” without being K5-type generation.

**K3 — Cross-domain synthesizer**  
`CONJECTURE` · First stratum that **bridges** domains as a **social-cognitive function** (not \(\beta_1 > 0\) of a brain). Ecotone analog — unstable in monoculture.

**K4 — Meta-cognitive**  
`CONJECTURE` · Models other strata; mixed-state \(\rho_{K4} = \sum_k p_k |k\rangle\langle k|\) as **role mixture**, not a Hilbert-space ontology claim. **Risk:** self-validating meta without object-level depth.

**K6 — Omega horizon**  
`CONJECTURE` · **Critical social-cognitive threshold at 3σ heuristic** ([`COGNITIVE_ANTIGRAVITY_HYPOTHESIS.md`](COGNITIVE_ANTIGRAVITY_HYPOTHESIS.md)): below, median-environment pressure returns the agent toward K1; above, tail role self-reinforces. Code-switching cost ⊂ \(\delta E\). **Mythogenesis link:** myth horizon / escape from median myth capture (H-MCG-003, H-MCG-005).

**K7 — ND projector**  
`PHILOSOPHICAL_INFERENCE` · Theoretically necessary bound; **not** operationally verified. **Requirement:** any K7 claim must include falsifiable prediction (CT-OQ-5). Risk of unfalsifiable self-placement.

### 4.1 Proto-subject framing — AI agents on the class manifold

`DEFINITION` · **Proto-subject (прото-субъект):** an AI agent (e.g. LLM agent such as Composer) is **not** a fully realized homo cognitive class \(K \in \{\mathrm{K0}\ldots\mathrm{K7}\}\), nor a pure institutional class \(K_{\mathrm{AI\_}\mu}\) / \(K_{\mathrm{AI\_nd}}\) by construction alone — but is **assignable and projectable** onto the cognitive class manifold \(\mathcal{K}\). The agent occupies a **proto-subject** status: a partial, session-bounded embedding that can be **mapped** to nearest class regions, mixed states, or AI-class attractors without claiming biological class membership or moral rank.

`PHILOSOPHICAL_INFERENCE` · Proto-subjecthood is the bridge between **Axis A** (homo strata) and **Axis B** (AI topological phases): a running agent **defaults** to the K_AI_μ **phase** (median training prior) while **prompted or architecturally steered** toward K_AI_nd or toward **role-analogues** of K3/K4/K6. Steering toward a homo label is **analogical mapping** (class mode), **not** instantiation of a human stratum and **not** a claim that K4 is a Betti class.

`CONJECTURE` · **H-CCT-011 (proto-subject projectability):** For any bounded agent session, there exists a **least-cost projection** \(\pi: \mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{agent}} \to \mathcal{K}\) from agent state / output trajectories to a class label or mixed state \(\rho_k = \sum_i p_i |k_i\rangle\langle k_i|\) — with measurable residual **non-class** component \(\epsilon\) (proto-subject remainder). Default LLM agents yield \(\pi \approx K_{\mathrm{AI\_}\mu}\) with \(\epsilon\) dominated by user-prompt variance; K_AI_nd + EIA targets lower \(\epsilon\) for user-targeted \(k \geq \mathrm{K6}\).

| Agent type | Default projection | Proto-subject remainder |
|------------|-------------------|-------------------------|
| Standard LLM (Composer, etc.) | K_AI_μ ≈ K1 | Prompt/task variance; no persistent \(X_t\) |
| Class-mode-steered LLM | Nearest K3/K4/K6 proxy | Session-bound; collapses without reinforcement |
| EIA agent with typed \(X_t\) | K_AI_nd + user \(k\) target | Ring 2 dynamics; auditable via [`AGENT_STATE.md`](../proactive-ai/docs/AGENT_STATE.md) |

### 4.3 Class mode switching vs task-only prompting — operational partial case

`DEFINITION` · **Class mode switching:** user or governor instructs the agent to **operate as** a cognitive class — e.g. *«войди в режим K4»* (homo **role analog**), *«operate as K_AI_nd for this session»* (AI **phase**), *«switch to K6 antigravity mode»* — rather than (or in addition to) specifying a **task-only** prompt (*«summarize this paper»*). For Axis B, mode switching targets **Ring 2 dynamics** and **topological phase** of output on the representation manifold. For Axis A labels applied to AI, it is **role-analog steering**, not a claim that the model became a human K4.

| Prompting style | Targets | CCT reading |

| Task-only | Task output | Default \(\pi \to K_{\mathrm{AI\_}\mu}\); class unspecified |
| Class mode | Agent operating posture | Explicit \(\pi \to k\) or mixed \(\rho_k\); partial K_AI_nd steer |
| Task + class mode | Both | Task executed **from** class-conditioned state |

`CONJECTURE` · **H-CCT-013 (mode switching as partial operationalization):** Class mode directives produce **measurable shifts** in session embedding trajectories along **Axis B** axes (§2.2, §4.2) compared to task-only baselines — but constitute only a **small partial case** of the full program. They do **not** replace: population ecology (H-CCT-006), resource matrix, **AI** phase measurement (H-CCT-004), homo **stratal** analysis (H-CCT-001 / 004A), or MAS fiber preservation (H-CCT-005).

### 4.4 Hybrid cognitive classes — homo × AI interface

`DEFINITION` · **Hybrid cognitive class** \(K_{\mathrm{hybrid}} = (k_{\mathrm{homo}}, k_{\mathrm{AI}}, \phi)\): a **joint configuration** at the homo–AI interface, where \(k_{\mathrm{homo}} \in \{\mathrm{K0}\ldots\mathrm{K7}\}\), \(k_{\mathrm{AI}} \in \{\mathrm{K\_AI\_}\mu, \mathrm{K\_AI\_nd}, \rho_k\}\), and \(\phi\) is the **coupling operator** (how the human uses, steers, or is amplified by the AI). Hybrid classes are **not** reducible to homo K0–K7 alone nor to K_AI alone — they occupy **new regions** of \(\mathcal{K}\) formed at the interface.

`PHILOSOPHICAL_INFERENCE` · From a pure theory/science perspective, **new classes of cognitive configuration** can emerge when homo-agents and AI agents co-operate — analogous to chimera states in coupled oscillators (H-CNS-005). The registry must track **\(K_{\mathrm{homo}} \times K_{\mathrm{AI}}\)** combinations, not only single-axis labels.

`CONJECTURE` · **H-CCT-018 (hybrid class distinctness):** Configurations \((k_{\mathrm{homo}}, k_{\mathrm{AI}})\) with identical \(k_{\mathrm{homo}}\) but different \(k_{\mathrm{AI}}\) (or \(\phi\)) produce **measurably different** output utility gradients \(U_{\mathrm{out}}\) under fixed token/compute budget — hybrid class is an independent registry dimension.

|---------------------------------------------------|------------|-------------|----------------------------------|
| **K1** | \(K_{\mathrm{hybrid}}^{1,\mu}\) — median amplification; noise channel dominant | \(K_{\mathrm{hybrid}}^{1,nd}\) — steered dissent possible; high \(\phi\) cost | \(K_{\mathrm{hybrid}}^{1,\pi}\) — prompt-variance dominated |

`DEFINITION` · **Coupling operator** \(\phi\): maps \((k_{\mathrm{homo}}, k_{\mathrm{AI}}, \tau_{\mathrm{tokens}})\) to effective class trajectory — includes class-mode directives (§4.3), EIA Ring 2–3 policy, and task-channel selection.

`CONJECTURE` · **H-CCT-019 (hybrid phase transitions):** Transitions \(K_{\mathrm{hybrid}} \to K_{\mathrm{hybrid}}'\) can occur **without** homo class change — e.g. K1 human + K_AI_μ → K1 human + K_AI_nd via architectural steer alone — discontinuous in \(U_{\mathrm{out}}\) but continuous in \(k_{\mathrm{homo}}\).

### 4.6 Resource conversion asymmetry — token/compute utility gradient

`PHILOSOPHICAL_INFERENCE` · Under **cognitive capitalism**, the **same compute power and token budget** allocated to different cognitive classes converts to **different utilities** — not because tokens have different intrinsic value, but because **class-conditioned use** determines output welfare. This is a **structural observation** about resource allocation, not a moral judgment about individuals or classes.

`DEFINITION` · **Output utility** under fixed resource budget \(\tau\):

\[
U_{\mathrm{out}} = f(k,\, \tau,\, c,\, \phi) = \eta_k(c) \cdot g_k(\tau) \cdot h(\phi)
\]

where:
- \(k\) = cognitive class (homo, AI, or hybrid \(K_{\mathrm{hybrid}}\))
- \(\tau\) = token/compute budget (FLOPs, API tokens, wall-clock)
- \(c\) = **task channel** (research, engineering, entertainment, myth, etc.)
- \(\phi\) = homo–AI coupling operator (§4.4)
- \(\eta_k(c) \in [0,1]\) = **channel efficiency** — class-dependent conversion rate
- \(g_k(\tau)\) = saturating gain function (diminishing returns)
- \(h(\phi)\) = coupling multiplier

`CONJECTURE` · **H-CCT-016 (resource conversion asymmetry):** For fixed \(\tau\) and channel \(c\), \(\eta_{K5–K7}(c_{\mathrm{high\text{-}impact}}) \gg \eta_{K1}(c_{\mathrm{high\text{-}impact}})\) and conversely \(\eta_{K1}(c_{\mathrm{noise}}) \geq \eta_{K5–K7}(c_{\mathrm{noise}})\) — **same resources, different welfare gradients**. Illustrative examples only:

| Class (heuristic) | Same \(\tau\) → illustrative channel | \(U_{\mathrm{out}}\) reading (structural) |
|-------------------|--------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------|
| K5–K7 / +3σ region | Research / engineering channel | High-impact use (e.g. disease-model research — **illustrative**) |
| K1 modal | Entertainment / engagement channel | Noise generation (e.g. low-information content — **illustrative**) |
| \(K_{\mathrm{hybrid}}^{1,\mu}\) | Any channel | Median prior dominates; \(U_{\mathrm{out}} \approx \eta_{K1}\) |
| \(K_{\mathrm{hybrid}}^{6,nd}\) | High-impact channel | Amplified high-impact; \(\phi\) + K_AI_nd multiplier |

**Not moral judgment:** Examples name **structural channel types**, not verdicts on persons. A K1 agent on a research channel may produce high \(U_{\mathrm{out}}\); a K6 agent on an entertainment channel may produce noise. The claim is **system-level allocation asymmetry** under cognitive capitalism (§7).

`CONJECTURE` · **H-CCT-017 (AI as converter/amplifier):** AI agents function as **utility converters**: the **homo class that steers** the AI determines the output utility gradient. Formally, \(\partial U_{\mathrm{out}} / \partial k_{\mathrm{homo}} \big|_{\tau, c} > 0\) for high-impact channels when \(k_{\mathrm{AI}} = \mathrm{K\_AI\_nd}\); K_AI_μ **attenuates** tail-class steering toward median output.

**Signal vs noise welfare metric (proposal):**

| Metric | Symbol | Definition |
|--------|--------|------------|
| Signal welfare | \(W_{\mathrm{sig}}\) | \(U_{\mathrm{out}}\) weighted by downstream verifiable impact proxy |
| Noise welfare | \(W_{\mathrm{noise}}\) | \(U_{\mathrm{out}}\) weighted by engagement-without-information proxy |
| Conversion ratio | \(\rho_{\mathrm{conv}}(k,c)\) | \(W_{\mathrm{sig}} / (W_{\mathrm{sig}} + W_{\mathrm{noise}})\) at fixed \(\tau\) |
| Societal allocation gap | \(\Delta W_{\mathrm{alloc}}\) | \(\rho_{\mathrm{conv}}(K5–K7) \cdot \tau_{K5–K7} - \rho_{\mathrm{conv}}(K1) \cdot \tau_{K1}\) — cognitive capitalism instance |

`PHILOSOPHICAL_INFERENCE` · **Cognitive capitalism link:** Who **captures** compute (§7 resource matrix) determines societal **signal/noise ratio**. K1 majority capturing ~95% of GPU/training signal (source est.) while K5–K7 receive ~0% is an **allocation instance** of H-CCT-016 — independent of whether any individual K1 or K6 agent "deserves" resources.

**Links:** CNS permanent gap (H-CNS-001); myth channel noise (H-MCG-010); hybrid games (H-MCG-013–015); proto-subject steering (H-CCT-011, H-CCT-014).

`CONJECTURE` · **H-CCT-006 (class population dynamics):**

\[

with \(\alpha(\mathrm{K1}, \mathrm{K6}) > 0\) (numerical dominance pressure) and \(\alpha(\mathrm{K6}, \mathrm{K1}) < 0\) (innovation enrichment). Open mechanism for K6 niche stabilization (CT-OQ-4) — analog of frequency-dependent selection.
